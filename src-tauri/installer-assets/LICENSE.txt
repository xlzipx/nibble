Nibble
Capture, trim and share - only what you need.
by ZIPEEK

================================================================
ABOUT
================================================================

Everything for quick clips on Windows: screenshots and screen
recordings with drawing tools, video downloads from YouTube, X and
1000+ sites, trimming with a live preview and compression that fits
Discord (20 / 50 / 500 MB).

Built around ffmpeg with GPU acceleration (NVENC / AMF / QSV): it
converts to AV1 / HEVC / H.264 and maps HDR to SDR.
