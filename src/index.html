About
Nibble v1.1.0
Capture, trim and share — only what you need.

Everything for quick clips on Windows: screenshots and screen recordings with drawing tools, video downloads from YouTube, X and 1000+ sites, trimming with a live preview and compression that fits Discord. Converts on your GPU (AV1 / HEVC / H.264) and maps HDR to SDR.

Built with
  • Tauri 2 · Rust + WebView2
  • ffmpeg · NVENC / AMF / QSV / x264 / x265
  • yt-dlp · URL downloads
